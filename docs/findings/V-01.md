# V-01 — Overflow

**Chapter:** visual  
**Rule:** `overflow`  
**Spec:** [Overflow](https://morkeleb.github.io/clickmonkey/findings/V-01/)

Content leaks out of its card, table, or the viewport. It looks broken and can hide a control.

Document wider than the viewport past the scrollbar gutter, or a box leaking past its edge. Intended scroll panes and 320px reflow (WCAG 1.4.10) are not this id — 320 overflow is A-1.4.10.

This id is **stable**. Reports link here so T/V/Q classes do not shuffle when a soak ranks differently.
