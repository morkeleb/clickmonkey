# V-05 — Broken scanline

**Chapter:** visual  
**Rule:** `scanline`  
**Spec:** [Broken scanline](https://morkeleb.github.io/clickmonkey/findings/V-05/)

Repeating items or form chrome do not share an edge. Scanning becomes hunting.

List/table row edges, tab titles, form fields on a row, or a header vs its cells. Nested nav indent, masonry, and two-column details grids (each field already has a row partner) are not this.

This id is **stable**. Reports link here so T/V/Q classes (and A-2.1.1) do not shuffle when a soak ranks differently.
